## Candidate papers

### Paper A

Title: A Human-in-the-Loop Workflow for Screening Research Papers with Large Language Models

Abstract: This paper proposes a staged workflow for screening research papers using title, abstract, and metadata before full-text analysis. It compares human-only screening with AI-assisted screening on a small literature review task and discusses verification requirements.

### Paper B

Title: Knowledge Management Tools for Graduate Research Notes: A Review

Abstract: This review summarizes note-taking tools, tagging systems, and knowledge-base workflows for graduate students. It compares several software patterns but does not propose a paper selection method.

### Paper C

Title: Exporting Reading Notes from Markdown to Personal Knowledge Bases

Abstract: This tutorial-style paper describes a plugin for exporting Markdown reading notes into personal knowledge-base tools. It focuses on formatting, metadata, and import/export compatibility.

### Paper D

Title: Time Management Habits in Undergraduate Online Learning

Abstract: This study surveys undergraduate time management habits in online courses. It focuses on course completion and self-reported study habits rather than research paper reading or literature review workflows.
