# Sample group meeting handoff

This is an illustrative handoff based on the fictional sample papers.

## Recommended deep-read paper

Paper A: A Human-in-the-Loop Workflow for Screening Research Papers with Large Language Models

## Why this paper first

AI Watson places Paper A at the front of the reading queue because it directly addresses staged paper screening before full-text analysis. It is more useful for the current group meeting than general note-taking or time-management papers.

## Sections to inspect first

1. Workflow overview figure.
2. Methods section describing the screening stages.
3. Evaluation section describing the test set.
4. Limitations section discussing missed papers or verification risks.

## Possible advisor questions

1. How did the authors define a paper as relevant or irrelevant?
2. Did they compare AI-assisted screening with human-only screening?
3. What happens when the model wrongly rejects a useful paper?
4. Is the workflow suitable for small graduate projects, or only for large reviews?
5. Which parts require human verification?

## Safe slide claims after verification

- The paper proposes a staged screening workflow.
- The workflow separates first-pass screening from deeper full-text analysis.
- The workflow requires human verification before relying on screening decisions.

## Do not claim yet

- Do not claim that the workflow is generally superior unless the evaluation supports it.
- Do not claim that AI can replace human literature screening.
- Do not claim that all papers can be safely filtered from abstracts alone.
